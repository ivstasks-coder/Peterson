Frequently asked

Straight answers,
before you call.

Sizing, deposits, damage, lead times, delivery and returns. If your question is not here, the phone is answered Monday to Friday.

Ordering

How do I place an order?

Call the shop or send an email with your date, venue and table list. There is no online checkout — every order is confirmed by a person.

How far ahead should I book?

Three to six months for a peak-season Saturday, four to six weeks otherwise. Short notice is still worth a call.

Is there a minimum order?

No. We dress single tables for home dinners and two hundred for a gala.

Can I hold a date while I decide?

Yes. We hold stock against your date at no cost while you are choosing.

Money

Is there a deposit?

Yes, taken at confirmation and credited against the final invoice.

How do I pay?

Card, check, or Venmo at @PetersonLinen215. Standing accounts can be invoiced on net terms.

What if I cancel?

Tell us as early as you can. Cancellation terms are on your order confirmation and we are reasonable about genuine changes of plan.

Sizing and fabric

What size cloth do I need?

The sizing guide on the linens page covers every standard table. Or send your table list and we will size it for you.

Can I see the fabric first?

Swatches by mail, or book a shop visit and see them on a real table under real light.

Can you match a specific colour?

Send a hex code, a printed sample, or a ribbon. We match from stock or tell you honestly that we cannot.

Delivery and after

Where do you deliver?

Greater Boston, the South Shore and Cape Cod as standard. Elsewhere by arrangement.

Do I have to launder the linen?

No. Leave it where it falls and we collect it.

What if something is damaged?

Ordinary wear is expected. Burns, tears and permanent staining are charged at replacement cost, and we always tell you first.

Can I visit the studio?

Yes, by appointment Monday to Friday. The shop floor is in use, so no walk-ins.

Expert help

Have a date on the books?

Tell us the venue and the table count. We have dressed most rooms south of Boston and can tell you what works in yours before you commit to a colour.
