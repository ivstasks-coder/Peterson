Planning

Lead times and delivery.

When to book

Three to six months ahead for a Saturday between May and October. Four to six weeks is comfortable outside peak season. Short notice is worth a call — stock is held here, not ordered in.

Where we deliver

Greater Boston, the South Shore and Cape Cod as standard. Further afield by arrangement — tell us the venue and we will tell you straight away whether it works.

Cost

What moves a quote.

There is no price list on this page, because the same cloth costs differently depending on the job. Four things account for most of the difference.

Piece count

How many tables, and whether napkins, runners and chair covers come with them.

Fabric

Matte solids sit at the base. Sequin, velvet and specialty weaves cost more to hold and to clean.

Delivery window

A standard drop is straightforward. A narrow or out-of-hours load-in is not.

Venue access

Early load-ins, long carries and second-floor rooms without a lift all add time.

Questions

The ones we are asked most.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, taken when the order is confirmed and credited against the final invoice.

What happens if something is damaged?

Ordinary wear is expected and not charged for. Burns, tears and permanent staining are charged at replacement cost, and we tell you before we invoice.

Can I change the count after booking?

Up to a week before the event, yes. After that, call us — it is usually still possible.

What if we do not use everything?

Unused pieces returned in their original packaging are credited.

Do you set the tables?

We deliver pressed and packed by table. Setting is usually the venue or caterer, but ask and we will tell you who does it at your venue.

What if the size is wrong?

If we sized it, we fix it — at our cost, on the day where we can.

Expert help

Have a date on the books?

Tell us the venue and the table count. We have dressed most rooms south of Boston and can tell you what works in yours before you commit to a colour.
