How it works

Five steps, one phone call.

01

Send your table list

Call or email with the date, the venue, and how many tables of what shape.

02

We work out sizes

You get the cloth sizes and drops for the room, with colours that actually hold together.

03

Samples and holds

Swatches by mail or a shop visit. The date is held while you decide.

04

Pressed and delivered

Packed by table, labelled, and dropped at the venue on the load-in schedule.

05

We collect after

Leave the linen where it falls. No counting, no laundering, no return trip.

Expert help
