What we do

For the event you only plan once.

Most people renting linen for a personal event have never done it before. That is fine — it is the part of the job we are best at. Send a table list and we will tell you what sizes you need, what drops look right in that room, and which colours hold together under the venue lighting.

We hold the date while you decide, mail swatches so you can see the fabric against your flowers, and confirm final counts up to a week out.

How it runs

From first call to collection.

1 · Call or email

Give us the date, the venue, and roughly how many tables of what shape.

2 · Sizes and colours

We return cloth sizes and drops for your room, with colour combinations that work.

3 · Swatches or a visit

Samples by mail, or come to the Braintree shop and see them on a table.

4 · Hold and confirm

The date is held while you decide. Final counts confirmed a week out.

5 · Delivered and collected

Pressed, packed by table, dropped on the load-in schedule, collected after.

Questions we get

Before you call.

How far ahead should I book?

Three to six months for a Saturday in peak season, less outside it. If your date is sooner, call anyway — we usually have stock.

Can I see the fabric first?

Yes. Swatches by mail, or book a shop visit and we will set them on a real table.

What if my count changes?

Tell us up to a week before the event. Small changes after that are usually still possible.

Do I have to launder anything?

No. Leave the linen where it falls and we collect it.

Expert help

Have a date on the books?

Tell us the venue and the table count. We have dressed most rooms south of Boston and can tell you what works in yours before you commit to a colour.
