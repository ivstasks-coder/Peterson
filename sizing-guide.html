Worth knowing before you order

Looking for something specific?

If you want a particular fabric or color, send a swatch and it gets searched for. For anything genuinely special, start about four months out — that’s how long it can take to produce a specific look.

Ordering chair covers?

Say what style of chair your venue uses. Banquet chairs, chiavari and folding chairs all need different covers, and “chairs” on an order form has caused more day-of problems than any other detail.

Anything that needs installing

Chair covers, valancing — should be scheduled ahead, so there’s enough time on site to finish it properly.
