How a referral works

Four steps, no portal.

01

Send it over

Call or email with the client’s name, the date and the venue. No forms, no login.

02

We quote

We reach the client, work out sizes and colours, and hold the linen for the date.

03

We deliver

Pressed, packed by table, dropped at the venue on the load-in schedule and collected after.

04

You are paid

Your fee goes out after the event settles, by check or Venmo, with the event named on it.
